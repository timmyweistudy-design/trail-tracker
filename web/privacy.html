隱私權政策

循徑拾光 Gather the Trail　·　最後更新：2026-10-03

English version below ↓　·　使用條款 Terms

本政策說明「循徑拾光」App（下稱本 App）如何蒐集、使用與保護你的資料。我們的原則很簡單：只蒐集讓 App 正常運作所需的資料，不販售你的個人資料。

一、我們蒐集哪些資料

位置資料

  • 你按下「開始記錄」時，App 會讀取 GPS 位置來記錄路徑、里程、爬升、步數與卡路里。若你開啟背景記錄，螢幕關閉時仍會持續記錄，直到你按結束。
  • 位置資料預設只存在你的手機本機；登入後可選擇備份到雲端（見下）。不記錄時不會取得你的位置。

帳號資料（僅在你登入社群功能時）

  • 以 Email 或 Google 登入時，我們透過 Supabase 儲存你的 Email、顯示名稱、帳號代號與頭像。

你建立的內容

  • 社群貼文、相片、留言、夥伴（寵物）、小隊等，是你主動建立並選擇分享的內容。

安全與社群功能（你使用時才會有）

  • 留守人：你設定的預計下山時間、步道、留守人名單，以及記錄中定時回報的最後位置。只有你選的留守人看得到；超時時會通知他們並附上最後位置。行程結束後就不再更新。
  • 山友路況回報與檢舉：你送出的路況類型、文字、照片與步道；檢舉時的理由。
  • 步道人氣：登入後走完一條步道，App 會送一筆「哪條步道、幾點出發、走多久」，不含軌跡與位置。對外只顯示匿名的統計數字（少於 3 人不顯示），可在設定關閉。
  • 山社、小隊：你加入的山社與小隊、成員身分；小隊同行時你的即時位置只給同隊的人看，不會存檔。
  • 錯誤紀錄：登入後若 App 發生程式錯誤，會自動送出錯誤訊息、App 版本與瀏覽器／裝置型號，用來修 bug，保存 30 天。
  • 推播：你開啟通知時，會儲存這台裝置的推播識別碼，用來送通知。

裝置本機資料

  • 行程紀錄、收藏、設定、離線地圖圖磚等，儲存在你的裝置上，用來讓 App 離線也能運作。

二、我們如何使用這些資料

  • 記錄與呈現你的健行路徑、統計與成就。
  • 提供社群、小隊同行、好友互動等功能（依你的分享設定）。
  • 登入後提供跨裝置的雲端備份與還原。

三、資料儲存與第三方服務

  • Supabase：帳號驗證、資料庫、雲端備份與相片儲存。
  • Google：僅在你選擇用 Google 登入時，用於身分驗證。
  • 地圖與地形服務（如 Esri、AWS 地形圖磚）：載入地圖圖磚，過程中會傳送地圖檢視區域的請求。
  • 翻譯與周邊地點服務：當你使用翻譯或查看步道周邊設施時，會向對應服務發出請求（MyMemory 翻譯、Google Places）。
  • 天氣與生態：Open-Meteo（天氣）、iNaturalist（附近物種目擊）只會收到步道的座標，不會收到你的身分。
  • 付款：訂閱由 Apple App Store（經 RevenueCat）或網頁版的 Stripe 處理，我們拿不到你的信用卡資料。

我們不會販售你的個人資料，也不會用於與 App 功能無關的廣告追蹤。

四、你的權利

  • 匯出：可在「我的 ▸ 資料備份」匯出你的行程備份檔。
  • 刪除：可清除本機資料；在「社群 ▸ 我的 ▸ 設定 ▸ 刪除帳號」可自行永久刪除雲端帳號與所有資料，也可以來信請我們處理。
  • 權限：位置權限可隨時在系統設定關閉；關閉後將無法記錄路徑。

五、兒童

本 App 適合含親子步道的一般大眾使用，但不特別針對 13 歲以下兒童蒐集個人資料。

六、聯絡我們

對隱私權有任何疑問，或要求刪除資料，請來信：gatherthetrail@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Gather the Trail (循徑拾光)　·　Last updated: 2026-10-03

This policy explains how the Gather the Trail app (“the App”) collects, uses and protects your data. Our principle is simple: we only collect what the App needs to work, and we do not sell your personal data.

1. Data we collect

Location

  • When you tap “Start recording”, the App reads your GPS location to record your route, distance, ascent, steps and calories. If you enable background recording, it keeps recording with the screen off until you stop.
  • Location data is stored on your device by default; once signed in you may back it up to the cloud (below). We do not access your location when you are not recording.

Account (only if you sign in)

  • When you sign in with Email or Google, we store your email, display name, handle and avatar via Supabase.

Content you create

  • Community posts, photos, comments, buddy (pet) and team data that you choose to create and share.

Safety & community features (only when you use them)

  • Check-in contact — your expected return time, trail, chosen contacts and, while recording, your periodically reported last position. Only your chosen contacts can see it; if you are overdue they are notified with your last position. Updates stop when the trip ends.
  • Trail reports & flags — the report type, text, photo and trail you submit, and the reason when you flag something.
  • Trail crowd levels — when signed in and you finish a trail, the App sends “which trail, start time, duration”, without your track or location. Only anonymous totals are shown (hidden under 3 people). You can turn this off in settings.
  • Clubs & teams — clubs and teams you join and your role; during team live-tracking your live position is shared only with your team and is not stored.
  • Error logs — when signed in, App errors are sent automatically with the app version and browser/device model to help fix bugs; kept for 30 days.
  • Push — if you enable notifications, we store this device’s push token to deliver them.

On-device data

  • Trip history, favorites, settings and offline map tiles are stored on your device so the App works offline.

2. How we use it

  • To record and display your hikes, statistics and achievements.
  • To provide community, team live-tracking and friend features (per your sharing settings).
  • To provide cross-device cloud backup and restore when signed in.

3. Storage & third parties

  • Supabase — authentication, database, cloud backup and photo storage.
  • Google — used for sign-in only, if you choose Google login.
  • Map & terrain services (e.g. Esri, AWS terrain tiles) — to load map tiles for the area you view.
  • Translation & nearby-place services — requests are sent when you use translation or view amenities near a trail (MyMemory, Google Places).
  • Weather & wildlife — Open-Meteo (weather) and iNaturalist (nearby sightings) receive only the trail’s coordinates, not your identity.
  • Payments — subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store (via RevenueCat) or Stripe on the web; we never see your card details.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for ad tracking unrelated to the App.

4. Your rights

  • Export — export a backup of your trips in “Me ▸ Data backup”.
  • Delete — clear on-device data anytime; delete your cloud account and all its data yourself in “Social ▸ Me ▸ Settings ▸ Delete account”, or email us.
  • Permissions — you can turn off location permission in system settings at any time; recording will then be unavailable.

5. Children

The App is intended for a general audience (including family-friendly trails) and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

6. Contact

For any privacy question or a data-deletion request, email gatherthetrail@gmail.com
