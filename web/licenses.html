本 App 用到下列開放原始碼、字型、地圖圖資、資料與音效。感謝這些作者與機關。

一、地圖與圖資

來源用途授權／條件
內政部國土測繪中心 臺灣通用電子地圖底圖；離線地圖只存開放資料版（EMAP5_OPENDATA，最細到縮放 15）開放資料版依政府資料開放授權條款第 1 版；縮放 16 以上為線上瀏覽「國土測繪圖資服務雲」圖層，不存到裝置。© 內政部國土測繪中心
Esri World Imagery、World Hillshade衛星影像、地形陰影透過 ArcGIS Location Platform 授權金鑰使用。© Esri 及其資料提供者
AWS Terrain Tiles（Mapzen terrarium）3D 地形高度開放資料；來源含 SRTM、GMTED、ETOPO1 等。© Mapzen、Amazon Web Services 及各資料來源
OpenStreetMap部分步道路線、沿線地標© OpenStreetMap 貢獻者，ODbL 1.0。App 內由 OSM 衍生的步道與地標資料同樣以 ODbL 1.0 提供，如需取得請來信。

二、資料與服務

來源用途授權／條件
林業及自然保育署 臺灣山林悠遊網、各縣市政府開放資料步道名稱、介紹、路況、照片政府資料開放授權條款第 1 版；照片依各頁標示
Google 地圖（Google Places）步道周邊美食、景點、停車／廁所／超商依 Google 地圖附加服務條款與 Google 隱私權政策；只列清單、不畫在非 Google 的地圖上
Open-Meteo天氣預報天氣資料 Weather data by Open-Meteo.com，CC BY 4.0
iNaturalist附近物種目擊與照片每張照片依原作者選的 Creative Commons 授權，並標示作者
Wikimedia Commons部分步道與物種照片依各檔案的授權（多為 CC BY／CC BY-SA），並標示作者
Open Topo Data（SRTM 30m）沒有高度的路線補算海拔SRTM 為公有領域資料；服務由 opentopodata.org 提供
MyMemory（Translated）貼文翻譯依 MyMemory 服務條款

Third-party licenses & data sources

The App uses the open-source code, fonts, map tiles, data and sounds below. Thank you to their authors and agencies. License texts are in the Chinese section above.

Maps

  • NLSC Taiwan e-Map (© National Land Surveying and Mapping Center) — base map. Offline maps store only the open-data layer EMAP5_OPENDATA (up to zoom 15) under the Open Government Data License v1.0; zoom 16+ is viewed online only and not stored on the device.
  • Esri World Imagery & World Hillshade — via an ArcGIS Location Platform key. © Esri and its data providers.
  • AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen terrarium) — 3D terrain; sources include SRTM, GMTED and ETOPO1.
  • OpenStreetMap — © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Trail and waypoint data derived from OSM is likewise available under ODbL 1.0 on request.

Data & services

  • Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency and county open data — trail info and photos (Open Government Data License v1.0; photos as credited).
  • Google Maps (Google Places) — nearby food, attractions and amenities, under the Google Maps Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Shown as lists only, never on a non-Google map.
  • Open-Meteo — Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, CC BY 4.0.
  • iNaturalist and Wikimedia Commons — photos under each author’s Creative Commons license, credited.
  • Open Topo Data (SRTM 30m, public domain) — elevation for routes without heights.
  • MyMemory — post translation, under its terms of service.

Fonts

  • Taipei Sans TC Beta (JT Foundry, from Adobe Source Han Sans) and Noto Serif TC Black — subsets, SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Sounds

The App currently uses no companion sounds, ambience or music (being reworked). Safety beeps while recording are generated in code.
